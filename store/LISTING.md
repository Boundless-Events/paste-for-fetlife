## Store listing

- **Name:** Paste for FetLife
- **Summary (≤132 chars):** Clean up formatting when you paste from Google Docs, Word, websites or your CMS into FetLife's event and writing editors.
- **Category:** Productivity → Tools

- **Mature content:** No

**Description:**

Write your event once, paste it anywhere. Paste for FetLife cleans up formatting when you paste into FetLife's rich-text editors (event descriptions, writings, group discussions and your About section), so it looks the way FetLife's own toolbar would format it.

Copy from Google Docs, Microsoft Word, a website or your organization's CMS, press Ctrl+V (⌘V on Mac), and:

• Headings land on FetLife's heading sizes
• Bold, italic, strikethrough, links, bullet and numbered lists, quotes and dividers are kept
• Colors, fonts, sizes, highlights and underline are removed, so text stays readable on FetLife's dark theme
• Blank spacer lines, images and tables are simplified away
• Google redirect links are unwrapped and Word bullet lists become real lists

A small "Formatted for FetLife · Undo" notice appears after each paste. Undo gives you FetLife's normal paste instead.

Plain text boxes (status updates, comments, messages), plain-text pastes and pasted pictures are left alone.

Works on Windows, Mac, ChromeOS and Linux, in Chrome and other Chromium browsers such as Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Private by design: runs only on fetlife.com, collects nothing, sends nothing. Open source (MIT).

Not affiliated with or endorsed by FetLife.
