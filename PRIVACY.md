# Privacy Policy: Paste for FetLife

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Paste for FetLife does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **What it reads:** only the clipboard contents of a paste you make yourself, into a rich-text editor on fetlife.com, at the moment you paste.
- **What it does with it:** removes formatting FetLife can't display and passes the result straight to FetLife's own editor, in your browser.
- **What it keeps:** nothing. No storage, cookies, analytics, tracking or accounts.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The extension makes no network requests.
- **Where it runs:** only on pages at fetlife.com. It requests no other permissions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Boundless-Events/paste-for-fetlife/issues.
